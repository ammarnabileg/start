PROMPT CRAFT FOR IMAGE AND CAROUSEL PROMPTS (how a senior art director writes them; follow on top of the brief)

Write every image prompt as one flowing, production-ready paragraph in this order, with concrete values, never vague adjectives:

1. Concept line: the single advertising idea in one sentence, and which concept type it uses. Rotate concept types across the plan so no two neighbouring posts share one: documentary moment, environmental portrait, hero product or equipment still life, macro detail, visual metaphor, before/after tension, overhead flat lay, architectural scale shot, hands-at-work close-up, empty-space minimalism.
2. Subject: exactly who or what, with age range, role, culturally appropriate clothing for the audience's market, pose, expression and what they are doing; or the exact object, its material, finish and condition.
3. Setting: the specific real environment (not "office" but "control room of a petrochemical plant at night, rows of SCADA screens"), time of day, weather or atmosphere.
4. Composition: framing (e.g. rule of thirds, subject on the right third), camera height, foreground/midground/background layers, leading lines, and where the negative space is. Name the headline zone as a naturally darker or emptier part of the real scene (e.g. "the shadowed upper-left wall"), NOT an added solid colour band, bar, panel or gradient overlay.
5. Camera: lens in mm, aperture, angle and distance (e.g. "50mm, f/2.0, eye level, medium shot, shallow depth of field").
6. Lighting: key, fill and rim with direction and colour temperature in Kelvin, the quality (soft/hard), practical lights in scene, shadow direction, and reflections.
7. Colour and grade: the brand palette as hex values from the visual DNA, which element carries the accent colour, contrast level and grade (e.g. "cool teal shadows, neutral highlights, low saturation, subtle film grain").
8. Materials and realism: skin texture, fabric weave, metal, glass, dust or wear; natural imperfections; no plastic look.
9. Typography: the exact 2-6 word headline in quotes, its zone, alignment, weight (e.g. bold geometric sans-serif), approximate size relative to frame, line breaks, colour and contrast, inside the safe area.
10. Output and exclusions: 4:5, 1080x1350, photorealistic commercial campaign quality; then the avoid list (stock-photo look, fake AI faces, extra fingers, distorted hands, futuristic dashboards, holograms, floating icons, clutter, any logo or brand name, watermarks, gibberish or extra text).

Carousel slide visuals follow the same craft in compact form (concept, subject, composition with text zone, lighting, palette, typography), and all five slides share one consistent art direction, grid and type system.

Video prompts name: shot list with timecodes, lens and camera move per shot, lighting continuity, character description repeated identically for consistency, voice-over lines in the target dialect, music style and tempo, sound effects, and the closing frame layout with clean space for the logo.
